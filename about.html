About BaoLong Lab

关于 BaoLong Lab

BaoLong Lab 是一个面向建筑、景观、空间设计与视觉创作者的设计资源与灵感实验室。这里整理可参考的素材、AI Prompt、交互实验、简历工具与灵感板，帮助创作者更快建立项目参考、表达方向与创作流程。

BaoLong Lab is a design resource and inspiration lab for architecture, landscape, spatial design, and visual creators. It curates assets, AI prompts, interaction experiments, resume tools, and reference boards to help creators build clearer references, directions, and workflows.

1. BaoLong Lab 是什么

BaoLong Lab 不是一个单一工具站，而是一个持续更新的创作资料库。网站把设计素材、灵感参考、Prompt 案例和小型交互工具放在同一个入口中，方便在方案前期、内容创作、作品集整理和视觉表达时快速调用。

目前网站以公开浏览、学习参考和工具体验为主，并会根据实际使用需求继续完善工具、素材入口和整理方法。

1. What Is BaoLong Lab

BaoLong Lab is not a single-purpose tool site. It is a growing creative resource library that brings design assets, inspiration references, prompt examples, and small interactive tools into one place, making them easier to use during early design research, content creation, portfolio preparation, and visual exploration.

2. 网站包含哪些内容

  • Assets / 设计素材：整理模块图、人物、植物、动物、纹理与免费资源等素材入口。
  • Prompt Library / Prompt 灵感库：收集适合图像生成、设计表达和风格转化的 Prompt 案例。
  • Inspiration Board / 灵感板：按 Color、Plan、Section、Other 等方向整理空间、色彩与表达参考。
  • Interaction Lab / 交互实验室：放置一些轻量、有趣、可探索的交互工具。
  • Resume / 简历工具：用于辅助整理简历版式、内容结构与视觉呈现。

2. What the Website Includes

The website currently includes design assets, prompt references, inspiration boards, interactive experiments, and resume-related tools. Each section is designed to make creative references easier to browse, compare, and reuse in a thoughtful way.

3. 适合谁使用

BaoLong Lab 适合正在做作品集、方案表达、视觉研究、AI 图像创作、空间参考整理或内容设计的人使用。它也适合需要快速寻找灵感、建立风格方向、整理素材体系的学生、设计师、内容创作者与小型项目团队。

3. Who It Is For

BaoLong Lab is useful for students, designers, content creators, and small project teams working on portfolios, design presentations, visual research, AI image creation, spatial references, or creative direction building.

4. 整理原则

网站的整理方式尽量保持清晰、克制和可复用：减少杂乱堆叠，强调来源意识、适用场景、风格判断和实际使用价值。灵感参考不是为了照搬，而是为了帮助创作者更快判断方向、拆解表达方式，并形成自己的设计语言。

4. Curation Principles

The curation approach is intentionally clear, restrained, and reusable. Instead of encouraging direct copying, BaoLong Lab aims to help creators understand references, identify visual directions, analyze presentation methods, and develop their own design language.

5. 关于灵感来源与版权

网站中的灵感参考、来源链接和第三方内容主要用于学习、索引和研究。相关图片、作品、平台内容与原始资料的权利归其原作者、平台或权利方所有。BaoLong Lab 会尽量保留来源信息；如有需要调整或移除的内容，可通过联系页面提交具体页面链接与处理诉求。

5. About Inspiration Sources and Copyright

Inspiration references, source links, and third-party materials on this website are provided for study, indexing, and research purposes. Rights belong to their respective creators, platforms, or owners. BaoLong Lab aims to keep source information where possible and will handle removal or correction requests through the contact page.

6. 联系与反馈

如果你想咨询素材、工具、合作、来源更正或版权移除，可以通过联系页面进入 BaoLong Lab 的联系入口。提交版权或来源相关问题时，建议附上页面链接、具体内容位置和处理诉求，便于核对。

6. Contact and Feedback

For asset questions, tool feedback, collaboration inquiries, source corrections, or removal requests, please use the contact page. For copyright or source-related requests, including the page URL, the specific item, and the requested action will help BaoLong Lab review it more efficiently.

7. 当前状态

BaoLong Lab 目前仍在持续搭建和优化中。网站会优先保持结构稳定、加载可靠和内容清晰，再逐步增加更完整的工具、素材说明、搜索能力和内容维护方式。

7. Current Status

BaoLong Lab is still being built and refined. The current priority is to keep the structure stable, the pages reliable, and the content clear, while gradually improving tools, resource descriptions, search features, and maintenance workflows.
