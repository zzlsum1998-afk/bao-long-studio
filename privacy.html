隐私政策Privacy Policy

本页面说明 BaoLong Lab 在网站访问、数据统计、第三方链接与用户主动提供信息时的基础处理方式。当前版本为基础说明，后续如开放账号、邮箱登录、上传或付费功能，会继续更新。

This page explains how BaoLong Lab handles basic website visits, analytics, third-party links, and information voluntarily provided by users. This is a basic version and may be updated when account, email login, upload, or payment features are introduced.

1. 我们收集哪些信息What we collect

当你访问 BaoLong Lab 时，网站可能通过访问统计工具收集基础访问数据，用于了解网站是否有人访问、页面打开情况、访问来源和设备环境。

When you visit BaoLong Lab, the site may use analytics tools to collect basic visit data, such as whether pages are visited, where visits come from, and what device environment is used.

  • 页面浏览、访问时间、访问来源、停留情况等统计信息；
  • 浏览器、设备类型、操作系统、屏幕尺寸等基础环境信息；
  • 由统计服务生成的匿名或半匿名访问标识。

这些信息主要用于网站运营分析和页面优化，不用于直接识别具体个人身份。

This information is mainly used for website operation analysis and page optimization. It is not used to directly identify a specific individual.

2. 百度统计Baidu Tongji analytics

本站使用百度统计（Baidu Tongji）了解网站访问情况。百度统计可能会通过 Cookie 或类似技术记录访问数据，并生成访问报表。

This site uses Baidu Tongji to understand website traffic. Baidu Tongji may use cookies or similar technologies to record visit data and generate analytics reports.

我们使用这些数据判断哪些页面被访问、用户大致从哪里进入、网站改版是否有效，以及是否存在页面访问异常。

We use these reports to understand which pages are visited, approximate traffic sources, whether website updates are effective, and whether there are unusual access patterns.

3. 用户主动提供的信息Information you voluntarily provide

当前 BaoLong Lab 主要是公开浏览型网站。若你通过未来的表单、邮箱、登录、上传工具或其他方式主动提供信息，我们会仅在完成对应功能、回复请求、改进服务或必要的安全维护范围内使用。

BaoLong Lab is currently mainly a public browsing website. If you voluntarily provide information through future forms, email, login, upload tools, or other channels, we will use it only to provide the relevant feature, respond to requests, improve services, or maintain necessary security.

在正式开放账号、上传、邮箱登录或付费功能前，我们会补充更完整的用户信息处理说明。

Before account, upload, email login, or payment features are officially launched, we will add more detailed information handling terms.

4. 第三方链接Third-party links

本站可能包含淘宝、Pinterest、作品来源、素材来源或其他第三方网站链接。点击这些链接后，你将离开 BaoLong Lab，相关网站如何收集和使用信息，应以对应第三方平台的政策为准。

This site may include links to Taobao, Pinterest, source references, asset references, or other third-party websites. Once you leave BaoLong Lab, the privacy practices of those third-party platforms apply.

5. Cookie 与本地存储Cookies and local storage

部分页面可能使用 Cookie、本地存储或浏览器缓存来保存语言偏好、工具状态、页面交互状态或访问统计信息，以便提升使用体验。

Some pages may use cookies, local storage, or browser cache to save language preferences, tool states, interaction states, or analytics information to improve the browsing experience.

6. 政策更新Updates

本政策可能会随着网站功能变化而更新。更新后会在本页面显示新的说明内容。

This policy may be updated as website features change. Updated information will be shown on this page.
