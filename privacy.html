隐私政策

本页面说明 BaoLong Lab 在网站访问、数据统计、第三方链接与用户主动提供信息时的基础处理方式。当前版本为基础说明，后续如开放账号、邮箱登录、上传或付费功能，会继续更新。

This page explains how BaoLong Lab handles basic website visits, analytics data, third-party links, and information that users may voluntarily provide. This is a basic version and will be updated if account, email login, upload, or payment features are introduced.

1. 我们收集哪些信息

当你访问 BaoLong Lab 时，网站可能通过访问统计工具收集基础访问数据，用于了解网站是否有人访问、页面打开情况、访问来源和设备环境。

  • 页面浏览、访问时间、访问来源、停留情况等统计信息；
  • 浏览器、设备类型、操作系统、屏幕尺寸等基础环境信息；
  • 由统计服务生成的匿名或半匿名访问标识。

这些信息主要用于网站运营分析和页面优化，不用于直接识别具体个人身份。

English

When you visit BaoLong Lab, we may collect basic analytics data such as page views, visit time, traffic source, device type, browser, operating system, screen size, and anonymous or semi-anonymous identifiers generated by analytics services. This information is used for website operation and improvement, not for directly identifying individual users.

2. 百度统计

本站使用百度统计（Baidu Tongji）了解网站访问情况。百度统计可能会通过 Cookie 或类似技术记录访问数据，并生成访问报表。

我们使用这些数据判断哪些页面被访问、用户大致从哪里进入、网站改版是否有效，以及是否存在页面访问异常。

English

This website uses Baidu Tongji to understand website traffic. Baidu Tongji may use cookies or similar technologies to record visit data and generate reports. We use these reports to understand which pages are visited, where visits roughly come from, whether updates are effective, and whether any page behaves abnormally.

3. 用户主动提供的信息

当前 BaoLong Lab 主要是公开浏览型网站。若你通过未来的表单、邮箱、登录、上传工具或其他方式主动提供信息，我们会仅在完成对应功能、回复请求、改进服务或必要的安全维护范围内使用。

在正式开放账号、上传、邮箱登录或付费功能前，我们会补充更完整的用户信息处理说明。

English

BaoLong Lab is currently mainly a public browsing website. If you voluntarily provide information through future forms, email, login, upload tools, or other features, we will use it only to provide the corresponding function, respond to requests, improve the service, or maintain necessary security. More detailed terms will be added before account, upload, email login, or paid features are formally launched.

4. 第三方链接

本站可能包含淘宝、Pinterest、作品来源、素材来源或其他第三方网站链接。点击这些链接后，你将离开 BaoLong Lab，相关网站如何收集和使用信息，应以对应第三方平台的政策为准。

English

BaoLong Lab may contain links to Taobao, Pinterest, source references, material sources, or other third-party websites. Once you click those links, you leave BaoLong Lab. The way those third parties collect and use information is governed by their own policies.

5. Cookie 与本地存储

部分页面可能使用 Cookie、本地存储或浏览器缓存来保存语言偏好、工具状态、页面交互状态或访问统计信息，以便提升使用体验。

English

Some pages may use cookies, local storage, or browser cache to save language preferences, tool states, interaction states, or analytics information in order to improve the browsing experience.

6. 政策更新

本政策可能会随着网站功能变化而更新。更新后会在本页面显示新的说明内容。

English

This policy may be updated as the website changes. Updated information will be published on this page.
